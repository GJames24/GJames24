# George Martins

I'm George. By day I'm a product owner in financial technology: seven years building data products in wealth management, SQL by hand, and this past year forecasting products for capital planning. I still think a good question to the data beats a good opinion about it.

In the evenings I build things from the ground up with an AI at my side. The projects below are what came of it: an agentic world simulation run like a scientific instrument, with every model call costed and a regression bench gating every commit that touches its tooling; a fine-tuning pipeline baselined and sealed before its first training round; a [benchmark](https://github.com/GJames24/work/blob/main/glossary.md#the-cat-show), still in progress, that enters frontier models into the jobs the simulation demands and grades them blind; and the operating model those projects taught me, written down as rules, most of them bought with a named failure.

What I like most is the ambiguous end of a problem: the part where nobody has written the question yet, and the work is to find its shape, instrument it, and let the data refuse me when I'm wrong. Building with coding agents as pair developers (Claude Code, Codex) is how all of this gets done, and the craft of working with them (specs, evals, cost on the meter, a record that stays honest when the agents do most of the writing) is as much the subject here as the software.

I make electronic music as PRIMAL. I learned Ableton the same way I learned everything above: with an AI at my elbow, one question at a time.

## Work

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tilda** | An agentic world simulation run like a scientific instrument. One canon thread of 74 days and simulated days reaching day 102 across its [experimental arms](https://github.com/GJames24/work/blob/main/glossary.md#the-trunk-an-arm), every model call costed, a 670-check regression bench gating every commit that touches tooling. |
| **The Second Stamp** | Preparing to fine-tune an open 8B model to fill one of Tilda's seats. 60 hand-authored scenes sealed into a 48/12 development/holdout split, GPU runs behind an approval gate with hashed inputs, baselined before training. |
| **The Kitten Breeder** | The operating model extracted from Tilda: 27 chapters, most a rule bought with a named failure, and a starter kit new projects copy on day one. |
| **The Cat Show** | A model benchmark built on Tilda. Frontier models are entered into the jobs the simulation's work demands (reading a day's tape, refuting a claim, [refereeing blind](https://github.com/GJames24/work/blob/main/glossary.md#a-referee), building to a spec), judged against written standards with every verdict traced to a trial folder, and awarded per class. First show: Opus 5 against Opus 5.5. In progress. |

These repositories are private and will stay that way while the work is live. **[Case studies for each are here](https://github.com/GJames24/work)**, with a glossary. Curated public editions of parts of the work are planned; planmeter is the first.

**Something runnable:** [planmeter](https://github.com/GJames24/planmeter), a small usage-projection tool extracted from Tilda.

**Field notes:** short pieces from inside Tilda in the [work](https://github.com/GJames24/work) repo's `field-notes` folder, each a product decision, a postmortem or a provenance story with its receipts: a phone line in every home, a busker's stipend and a machine on the stage, and the mail carriers' four root causes.
